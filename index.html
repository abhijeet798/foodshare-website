The Crisis

Why FoodShare Matters

The world produces enough food to feed everyone, yet millions go to bed hungry every night.

🗑️

Massive Food Waste

Restaurants, households, and supermarkets discard billions of tons of perfectly edible food every year due to lack of coordination and awareness.

🍽️

Widespread Hunger

828 million people worldwide experience food insecurity daily, with many unable to access nutritious meals despite surplus food existing nearby.

🌍

Environmental Damage

Rotting food in landfills produces methane, contributing 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions and accelerating climate change.

🔗

No Efficient Bridge

Donors and recipients lack a reliable, real-time platform to connect quickly, resulting in missed opportunities to distribute surplus food.
